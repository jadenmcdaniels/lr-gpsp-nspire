gpSP for the TI-Nspire: the interface as of r19 (2026-11-21)
=============================================================

This file says what changed against docs/ui/spec.txt (r16-r18) and is the reference for the PC tests'
scripts. The code: gui.c (what the keys do), nspire_ui_screens.c (what is drawn), nspire_ui.c (the kit).

What r19 changed, in short: capitals only; a game's title that scrolls smoothly and comes round again; fewer
settings; the calculator's keypad drawn with each key's binding where it is; a path at the top of every page
that says how deep it is; the folders reached from the games themselves; one press from GAMES to QUIT; 7 9 1 3
doing nothing unless the player binds them; the volume in 20 steps (0/20 to 20/20); a setting with few values
changed by one press; one screen instead of a screen each for the games, the settings, the folders and quitting;
and every name under a picture saying what the picture is.

1. ONE SCREEN
-------------
(The first r19 had a row of seven tabs. It became one screen of items, each a picture with one name, a selector
in the top row, and the folders among the items instead of a menu of their own.)

Every top-level screen has the same top row (ui_nav, baseline 18): a word in each corner and the selector
between them, centred:

    with a game paused    SETTINGS            SAVES  GAMES            QUIT      (words 0-3)
    without a game        SETTINGS               GAMES                QUIT      (words 0-2)

Under it, the picked word's things; at the bottom, the band. There is no Resume, Save, Load, Link or Folders
tab and no screen of their own for any of them.

    GAMES      the games as items: a grid three by two, each a picture (its newest save's) with one name
               centred under it; after the games, the folders as items too (this folder's, "USB DRIVE" when
               there is one, "BACK" the way up, last): a folder's name with a slash ("GBC/"; USB DRIVE and
               BACK without one), no picture. v: a line an item.
    SAVES      the paused game's saves, the same grid: RESUME (the game as it stands), SAVE (a "+"), the game's
               own save ("IN-GAME SAVE", when it has one), then every state, the newest first.
    SETTINGS   the settings themselves: tiles three across, each a name over its value (VOLUME over 14/20), and
               at their end the screens that are lists (KEYS, BLUETOOTH) and MORE. No pages between (an earlier
               r19 had nine pages of a few tiles each).
    QUIT       one line: what quitting does to the game ("THE GAME IS SAVED FIRST.").

The arrows are either ON THE TOP ROW or IN THE BODY under the picked word. One black block on the screen says
where: on the row the picked word is the block (every word black); in the body the picked thing's name is the
block, its word in the row black and the others grey. The item the body is on stays in colour either way; the
others are black and white.

Keys, on the top row:
    left / right   the word before / after, wrapping. What is under a word shows as soon as it is picked.
    down, enter    into the body (GAMES, SAVES, SETTINGS); enter on QUIT quits gpSP.
    esc, menu      with a game: back to the game. Without one: the block goes to QUIT (no key quits by itself).
Keys, in the body:
    up from the top row of items     back to the top row
    esc, menu                        GAMES and SETTINGS: back to the top row. SAVES: back to the game at once (it
                                     is the paused menu's first screen: menu, then menu or esc again, resumes)
    GAMES      arrows move in the grid: left and right stop at the row's ends; down from the last row goes round
               to the first; up from the first row goes to the top row. enter plays the game (shift held:
               without resuming its last save) or opens the folder (the grid then shows that folder, the arrows
               on its first item). S the picked game's saves (a page: section 2), V grid/lines, tab moves a game
               among the games (shift+tab sorts A to Z), U the USB drive.
    SAVES      arrows as GAMES', but nothing wraps. enter on RESUME goes back to the game; on SAVE saves (to a
               new slot, or the same one: Settings > Saving > Save to) and goes back to the game; on a state
               loads it; on IN-GAME SAVE restarts the game from its own save. S on a state saves over it (after
               a question); S on RESUME or SAVE saves as SAVE does. D deletes (after a question, unless
               Confirm delete is off; del is the GBA's A as the keys come, so it is enter here as in every menu;
               a del that isn't a button's key deletes too), M marks (held: sweeps), A all, C/U pack/unpack, T
               copies to the other place, tab switches the place (calculator / USB drive) while a drive is in.
               After a question or a save that didn't happen the arrows are back on the tile they were on.
    SETTINGS   arrows move among the tiles (left and right: the one before or after, in order, going round;
               up and down: the row above or below, down from the last going round; up from the first row: the
               top row). enter changes the picked setting (section 3) or opens its screen; esc: the top row.
               Coming back into the tiles, the arrows are on the tile they left. The tiles, in order: VOLUME,
               BRIGHTNESS, SCREEN SIZE, FPS COUNTER, AUTOSAVE, KEYS, CONTROLLER, (BLUETOOTH: builds with it),
               (LINK: with a game), (CHEATS: a game with codes), MORE: eleven at most, four rows of three, so
               the screen never scrolls.

How gpSP opens: without a game, GAMES with the arrows in the grid, its first item picked. The menu key in a
game: SAVES with the arrows in its tiles, RESUME picked (enter, esc or menu go back to the game; right, enter
saves).

What the PC tests see (ui_shown): "main:N" whenever the arrows are on the top row (N the word's place: with a
game SETTINGS 0, SAVES 1, GAMES 2, QUIT 3; without one SETTINGS 0, GAMES 1, QUIT 2); "Settings:K" with the
arrows in Settings' tiles and "More:K" on its one page (K the picked tile's place among the tiles shown);
"list:K" in GAMES' grid (K the picked item's place: the games first, then the folders);
"saves:K" in SAVES' tiles and on a game's saves page (K the picked tile: with a game paused RESUME 0, SAVE 1,
then the game's own save if it has one, then the states); "Cheats:K"; "dialog:K", "keys:K" as before. The
About page is "About:0".

2. THE BAND, THE TILES AND THE HEADER
-------------------------------------
A tile is a picture (96 x 64, at x 8 / 112 / 216, rows from 30 and 126) and one name under it, centred, in
the bold weight; the picked one's name is white on a block as wide as the tile and 4 more either side (rows
65-82 under the picture's top). A name too long is cut ("..."), still centred; picked, it runs round: after a
second it moves left a pixel at a time (30 px a second) and comes in again from the right after a gap of 24 px
(ui_line_text, ui_scroll_after(ms, period)). A marked save says so in its name ("SLOT 4 MARKED").

The band (rows 222-239), on a screen of items: at its left what the picked item is, in the regular weight (a
game: "4 SAVES . 2 H", "NOT PLAYED"; a folder: "FOLDER", or "UP FROM <path>"; a state: "2 HOURS AGO . 13 KB";
RESUME: the game's name; SAVE: "TO SLOT 5"); at its right, before the battery, the one or two keys a player
needs there (the key bold, what it does regular): a game "S SAVES"; a state "S SAVE  D DELETE" (with a drive
in, "TAB USB DRIVE" too); on a game's saves page "D DELETE  M MARK". enter is never said, and V, tab, C, U, T
work without being said (the README lists them). What just happened takes the whole band instead ("DELETED 1
(13 KB).", "SLOT 3 IS EMPTY.").
Elsewhere the band is: what just happened, else the picked setting's one sentence, else the keys.
The battery ("82%") is the band's right end on every screen.

A page (anything opened from the body) has a path where the top row was: "SETTINGS / KEYS", "SETTINGS / MORE",
"SETTINGS / MORE / ABOUT", "SETTINGS / CONTROLLER / DETAILS", "SETTINGS / MORE / GAMES", "<GAME> /
SAVES" (another game's saves, from GAMES' S): the steps before the last in grey, the last black.

Text: capitals only (ui_text maps a-z to A-Z, whatever the code passes). One size, two weights. The left edge
is x 8 (UI_MARGIN). A list's picked line (the keys' list, Bluetooth's devices, the games' lines) is a block
from x 4 to 315.

3. SETTINGS
-----------
One screen under the top row, and one page behind its last tile (MORE). Neither scrolls. Every choice that only
decided speed is made for the player (the fast one is the default) and has no tile.

A tile is a name over its value, both centred; a tile that only opens something (KEYS, CONTROLLER, BLUETOOTH,
MORE, ABOUT) is its name alone, in the tile's middle (no arrow after it). Three across. Changing a value:
    a setting with up to four values    enter is the next value (one press: no edit state)
    a range (more than four values)     enter puts the value between < >, left/right change it (stopping at the
                                        ends), enter keeps it, esc puts it back
SETTINGS, in order (a tile in brackets is shown only when it applies):
    Volume (0/20-20/20, range; the band says what plays the sound) · Brightness (System, 1/10-10/10, range) ·
    Screen size (Original/Fit/Stretch; the band says what the picked one is: "THE GBA'S OWN PIXELS, WITH A
    BORDER." / "FILLS THE WIDTH. BARS ABOVE AND BELOW." / "FILLS THE SCREEN. A LITTLE TALLER.") · FPS counter
    (Off/On/Detailed) · Autosave (Off, 30 s ... 30 min, range) ·
    Keys · Controller · [Bluetooth] · [Link: its value the link's state (No cable/Ready/Waiting/Linked or
    Player N/Swap ends/Controller in), enter links or unlinks] · [Cheats: N codes] · More
MORE, in order:
    Frame skip (Auto/Off; Manual, from an older file, shows until it is changed) · Save to (Same slot/New slot:
    the save key's) · [Autosave to (Same slot/New slot): only with Autosave on] ·
    [Saves on (Calculator/USB drive): with a drive] · Link device (Auto/Wireless/Cable/Cable, exact) ·
    Settings for (All games/This game) · Keys for · [LCD fix (Off/On): older CX only] · [Game: Restart] ·
    Saves: Manage · Games: Manage · About                                (Game: only with a game)
    Saves: Manage opens the paused game's saves as a page (the save manager: mark, delete, pack, copy; the page
    GAMES' S opens for any game); without a game it says "IN GAMES, PRESS S ON A GAME." and opens nothing.
    Keys, Controller, Bluetooth, Cheats, the games' manager: screens of their own, as before.

No tile, the value FIXED whatever a settings file says (nspire_config_fast, nspire_stubs.c; on the calculator
always, in the PC build only with GPSP_HOST_FAST set, so that its tests can still drive the other modes through
settings files): Flicker fix (off: no frame mixing), Compatibility (Fast), Fast forward's top speed (none).
(Frame skip has its tile again, after a build that fixed it to Auto. The speed test is gone from the menu.)
Fixed the other way, for the picture: Fit is drawn SMOOTHED (the sharp Fit, scaled_raw, becomes scaled_aspect)
and Transparency is on.
No tile, the value as the files have it (a new file: the default in brackets): Sound delay [Short], Sound output
[Auto], BT quality [Auto], Turbo rate [15/s], CPU speed [Auto], Fast memory [on, CX II], Game clock [Auto], BIOS
[gba_bios.bin when it is there], Auto resume [on], Save on quit [on], Ask before deleting [on], Link speed [Fast].
CPU speed, Fast memory and Link speed still turn themselves down after a freeze, and stay down.
Gone since r18 the same way: Skip up to, Skip pattern, Sprite limit, Compress saves, In-game save's time, ROM
cache, the Sound switch (volume 0 is off) and Channels.
Renamed since r18: Size -> Screen size (Small -> Original, Full -> Stretch), Show FPS -> FPS counter (On, with ms -> Detailed), New slot per save ->
Save to, Link's Mode -> Link device (Wireless adapter -> Wireless, Link cable -> Cable).

4. KEYS
-------
Settings > Keys: the list at the left (ten lines on the screen: Up Down Left Right A B L R Start Select Menu
Fast forward, Save state, Load state, Load newest, Save & quit, Next slot, Prev slot, FPS counter, Restart
game, Brightness up, Brightness down, then Reset keys), each with its key at the right ("NO KEY" in grey when
it has none; nothing there when even that doesn't fit beside the name), and at the right the
calculator's keypad drawn in type: a key that does nothing is its own name in grey, a bound key says what it
does in black, the picked line's key is the black block. enter then a key sets it; del clears it; tab then a key
sets a GBA button's turbo (said in the band: "TAB TURBO =+DEL"). With a controller in, left/right switch between
the calculator's keys and the controller's (the controller's list is the whole width, no drawing).
In a game 7 9 1 3 do nothing unless the player binds them (they were the diagonals). 8 5 4 6 stay Up Down Left
Right by default; in the menus 8 2 5 4 6 still move as the arrows do.

5. SOUND
--------
Volume is 0 to 20 (nspire_audio_volume; "14/20"; the default 14, as loud as the old 70%): Settings' first tile.
There is no Sound page, and Sound delay, Sound output and BT quality have no tile (section 3). The * and / keys in a
game step it by one ("VOLUME 15/20"). Settings files: word 3 bit 21 says bits 16-20 hold it; bits 4-7 keep half
of it for older builds; a file without bit 21 gives twice its old 0-10. A game's own file: "volume20=N"; an old
"audio_volume=N" is read as 2N.

6. CHEATS
---------
(Cheats come from a file on the calculator or on a USB drive, and each code has a switch. Until r19 the engine
was in the build and nothing loaded a code into it.)

The file is RetroArch's .cht (cheatN_desc / cheatN_code / cheatN_enable lines; libretro-database has one a game).
Its name is the game's (its file's name without ".gba"), in any case, then ".cht" or ".cht.tns"; a tag in
brackets may come between ("Pokemon - Emerald Version (USA, Europe) (Code Breaker).cht"). It is looked for when
the game starts, in this order: the game's own folder (the calculator's or the drive's), the calculator's
zgpSP_files, then on a USB drive its top folder, "cheats" and "zgpSP_files". With no file loaded it is looked for
again whenever Settings is drawn and a drive has come or gone since.

The codes are CodeBreaker's ("XXXXXXXX YYYY", parts joined by "+" or spaces): the engine's (libretro-gpsp
cheats.c). An Action Replay or GameShark code ("XXXXXXXX YYYYYYYY") or a mistyped one is left out and counted.
At most 160 codes of a file are kept, and at most 20 are on at once.

Settings shows a CHEATS tile only for a game whose file was found; its value is how many are on ("2 ON"). A
file with no code the engine can run still has its tile, "NONE USABLE"; enter on it says "CODEBREAKER CODES ONLY:
NONE HERE." in the band and opens nothing.
enter opens the page (ui_shown "cheats:K", K the picked code): header "SETTINGS / CHEATS" with "2 ON" at its
right; a line a code, its name at the left and ON or OFF at the right (OFF grey); ten lines on the screen, more
scroll (a bar at the right). up/down move (going round), left/right move a screenful, enter turns the picked
code on or off (a 21st: "20 CODES ON AT MOST. TURN ONE OFF FIRST."), esc goes back to Settings. The band: "FROM
THE CALCULATOR." or "FROM THE USB DRIVE.", and "11 NOT SUPPORTED." when the file has codes the engine can't run.
Which codes are on is kept in the file itself (its cheatN_enable lines, written back when the page is left and
when the game is): there is no other file, and a file on a drive that has gone keeps its switches for the game
that is running only.

7. CONTROLLER
-------------
Settings > Controller is one list: a line a controller that is connected, its name at the left and USB or
BLUETOOTH at the right; with none, one sentence ("NO CONTROLLER. PLUG ONE INTO THE USB PORT, OR PAIR ONE IN
SETTINGS > BLUETOOTH."). No tiles and no word for the port's state: one list instead of four tiles, and no word
that flips between states while the port looks for a device.
When the port asks something of the player (a wrong cable end, no power, a device that isn't a controller) the
sentence for it is under the list once the state has been the same for two seconds. The band: "ENTER RESTART
USB  D DETAILS": enter restarts the port's power (what came of it is the header's note), D opens the details
(the port's registers, for a report; there right gives the OS thirty seconds), esc goes back. ui_shown is
"controller:<state>" on both.

8. BLUETOOTH (r19-bt)
---------------------
Settings > Bluetooth: its switch and SEARCH two tiles, the devices a list under them (PAIRED, FOUND), a line a
device: its name at the left, at the right what it is or what it is doing (CONTROLLER, HEADPHONES, OTHER, PAIRED,
CONNECTED, PAIRING, CONNECTING) and, only when its signal is weak (the lowest two of four steps, under -75 dBm),
" . WEAK" after it (the screens' middle dot between two facts). No "3/4": a signal step as a fraction told the
player nothing. A line PAIRING or CONNECTING (the player's, waiting its turn or under way, or
the last headphones' automatic reconnect): the band "ENTER STOP" (and "DEL FORGET" on a paired one); enter stops it
("STOPPED." takes the band, the line found or paired again), enter again starts it again. ui_shown "bluetooth:K", K
the picked line (the tiles 0 and 1, then the list's lines and headings).
Bluetooth LE controllers (the Xbox Wireless Controller) are listed and connect as the others. An LE device that is
not a controller: enter says "ONLY CONTROLLERS AND HEADPHONES CONNECT."; an LE controller on a dongle where LE does
not work: its line's sentence and enter's, "THIS DONGLE CAN'T CONNECT LE CONTROLLERS." (the old "BLUETOOTH LE DEVICES
AREN'T SUPPORTED." is gone).
